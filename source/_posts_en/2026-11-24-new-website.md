Welcome to our new website (also known as our marketing site).

We are super happy with it. It's full of funny panda images, with a lot of content to browse: from [our complete handbook](/en/handbook/) with dozens of pages of content, to [this blog](/en/blog/), to our fun [About page](/en/about/) - this was a very funny experience.

We've sweated the details, but I hope that it's bug-free too. If you see anything, leave us a note on [our official repository](https://github.com/monicahq/monica). Or even better - send us a [pull request](https://github.com/monicahq/marketing/pulls)!

For the nerds, the site is powered by Jigsaw, hosted on Cloudflare and is 100% static. So it should load super fast everywhere in the world. The homepage weighs around 430 KB, even with all those images, and we are almost at [100 on all four criteria in the PageSpeed index](https://pagespeed.web.dev/analysis/https-monicahq-com/ghmfi4ixvos5n27vlfxzc6?form_factor=desktop). The Largest Contentful Paint is around 0.6s. Glory to static sites.
