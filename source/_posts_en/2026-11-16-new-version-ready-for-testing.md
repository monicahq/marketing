We are happy to announce that the new version is ready for testing.

You can add your email to the waiting list and we will let you in. Keep in mind that it's a testing version and that it's not yet stable. Also, things will change every day, since we merge new code every day.

The plan for the coming days:

- we hope you will help us find and fix bugs,
- we will merge the new code into the Monica codebase on GitHub,
- we will launch a new marketing site (super proud of that),
- and finally, we will close all the old issues linked to previous versions of the product.

The waiting list is here: [https://my.monicahq.com/](https://my.monicahq.com/)
