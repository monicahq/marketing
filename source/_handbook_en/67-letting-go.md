Some relationships end. Some become less close. Some involve long silences that nobody finds particularly troubling.

Monica shouldn't look at the date of your last conversation and decide which of those stories is happening.

A gap is something the software can represent. Its meaning belongs to the people involved.

### No invented social debt

A reminder you choose can be useful. A system declaring that you owe someone contact because an arbitrary interval elapsed is making a much bigger assumption.

You may have stepped away for a good reason. You may be grieving. You may be protecting yourself. Or you might simply have a friendship that works perfectly well without weekly maintenance.

We shouldn't turn those situations into red warnings or a score that improves when you send enough messages.

Keeping a memory doesn't oblige you to resume a relationship either. A record can preserve something from your past without becoming a task for your future.

This is one consequence of [people not being sales leads](/en/handbook/sales-leads/). There is no universal objective of moving every contact towards more interaction.

Monica should help you follow your own intentions, including changing them. It shouldn't require an explanation for why you no longer want a reminder or why someone remains in your history without being in your present.

A personal CRM is already a slightly unusual idea. Let's avoid making it a tiny disappointed manager of your social life as well.
