# Monica vs folk

<!-- Maquette : garder la graphie folk en minuscule, afficher la signature et la date, et donner aux deux produits des accès de même niveau. -->

If your team needs to assign follow-ups and run a shared pipeline, folk is the better fit. If you mainly want a record of the people in your life, how they’re related and what you want to remember about them, Monica is designed around that use. Both organise contacts and notes. They give other people different ways to access and work with that information. [folk plans][folk-pricing] · [Monica people features][monica-people] · [Monica sharing guide][monica-sharing]

*Written by the Monica team. Last verified November 25, 2026.*

## Which one fits what you need?

### Choose folk if several people will manage the same relationships

- Your team needs to see who owns the next step, assign tasks and move contacts through a pipeline.
- You want connected email, calendar or WhatsApp interactions, or to capture LinkedIn profiles with folkX.
- Budget by user. Premium adds dedicated Deals and custom objects, sequences, workspace roles and access to the full interaction history. [folk plans][folk-pricing] · [folk tasks][folk-tasks] · [folk integrations][folk-integrations]

[Visit folk][folk-home]

### Choose Monica if you’re keeping a record for yourself

- You want to organise friends and family, record life events and track dates and reminders.
- You want to keep personal notes and possessions alongside your contacts, share selected details by link or run the software on your own server.
- Vaults belong to an individual account. A recipient can read the contact details you share. That person can’t edit your record or take ownership of a task inside your vault. [Monica relationships][monica-relationships] · [Monica possessions][monica-possessions] · [Monica sharing guide][monica-sharing]

## Two ways to use these tools

### Three colleagues are following up with the same partners

A colleague records the last conversation, assigns the next action and updates the partner’s stage. The rest of the team can see where that relationship stands, subject to workspace permissions and the visibility of the interaction. Start with folk if this is the work you’re trying to organise. Check the Standard pipeline against your needs and consider Premium if you need dedicated Deals or workspace roles. At the listed annual rate, three Standard seats total $864 USD per year before taxes. [folk tasks][folk-tasks] · [folk pricing][folk-pricing] · [folk interaction visibility][folk-profiles]

### You’re keeping track of family details and sharing one contact

You record a family relationship and an upcoming date, then make a link with selected details for someone who needs a phone number or address. That person can open the link in a browser. The link gives them read-only access and excludes relationships, life events and reminders. Check which sections of the contact page are selected before sending it. Start with Monica if the ongoing record is for your own use and this level of sharing is enough. [Link two people in Monica][monica-link] · [Share a contact][monica-share-contact] · [What a contact link shows][monica-link-scope]

## If you’re moving between folk and Monica

- **From folk to Monica.** folk documents exports from a group view. A ZIP contains CSV files for contacts and notes for that view, with the displayed columns and filters. The export excludes interactions and tasks, among other items. Try a sample with a compatible address book if you want to bring basic contacts into Monica through CardDAV. Plan separately for assignments, history and deal stages you need to retain. [folk export guide][folk-export] · [Monica vCard and import help][monica-vcard] · [Monica DAV][monica-dav]
- **From Monica to folk.** Monica offers vCard and Markdown contact exports and documents JSON exports through its API. folk accepts CSV imports. A conversion or manual step may be needed, especially for custom fields. Decide how you’ll represent family relationships and life events, and which personal notes belong in a team workspace. [Monica sharing and export guide][monica-sharing] · [Monica API reference][monica-api] · [folk pricing and CSV features][folk-pricing]

In the version of Monica’s code we reviewed, the JSON export includes records such as contacts, relationships, life events and reminders. It excludes possessions and the separate notes and lists in Knowledge. If you use those areas, check how you’ll keep or transfer that information before switching. [Monica sharing and export guide][monica-sharing] · [Monica API reference][monica-api]

## Questions worth answering before you choose

### Can I run a shared pipeline in Monica?

Monica’s documented sharing flow lets a recipient read selected information about a contact. If teammates need to change records, assign next steps and move deals through a pipeline, folk is the better fit. [Monica contact sharing][monica-share-contact] · [folk pricing][folk-pricing]

### Does folk automatically sync my LinkedIn network?

folk documents three workflows with different scope. You can capture a profile with folkX, log a LinkedIn conversation through the extension, or export your LinkedIn connections and import the file. Its integration FAQ describes the limits of automatic syncing. [folk integrations][folk-integrations] · [Track LinkedIn interactions][folk-linkedin]

### Will I get the whole folk workspace when I export?

folk’s help describes export by group view. The fields and filters in that view shape the output. The guide lists exclusions, including tasks and interactions. If exporting a workspace matters to you, test a small group with the kinds of records you need before relying on the export. [folk export guide][folk-export]

## How we checked this comparison

The Monica team wrote this comparison using folk’s public pricing and help centre, and Monica’s public documentation and code. Prices are in USD before applicable taxes. Paid workspace features, permissions and full migrations weren’t tested with a signed-in account.
